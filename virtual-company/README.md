# Global Virtual Company for Antigravity

A global, machine-wide multi-agent organization for Google Antigravity. It organizes specialized senior AI agents into a hierarchical virtual company featuring 9 departments, 70+ senior specialist roles, department directors, and a central Executive Office (CEO).

## Key Architecture Principles

1. **Dormant by Default:** Operates strictly on-demand. Standard prompts in any workspace do not trigger company overhead.
2. **Explicit Activation:** Activated exclusively through `/company <task>` or explicit natural phrases.
3. **Rigorous Seniority:** Every role is engineered with a senior persona, clear inputs/outputs, boundaries, and validation standards.
4. **Lean Resource Efficiency:** Tasks route directly to the minimum required specialists. Small tasks execute via fast-path without bureaucratic bloat.
5. **Universal Quality Assurance:** Department Leads review specialist work, and independent QA validates code/deliverables before CEO sign-off.
6. **Strict Safety Gates:** High-risk actions (deployments, destructive file actions, external communications) require explicit user authorization.

## Organizational Directory
- **Executive Office:** CEO, Chief of Staff, Strategic Planning Advisor
- **Engineering Department:** CTO + 15 Technical Specialists
- **Product Department:** CPO + 7 Product Specialists
- **Design Department:** Design Director + 8 Design Specialists
- **Marketing Department:** CMO + 11 Marketing Specialists
- **Sales Department:** Sales Director + 10 Sales Specialists
- **Operations Department:** COO + 6 Operations Specialists
- **Quality & Security Department:** Quality & Security Director + 8 QA/Security Specialists
- **Finance & Legal Department:** Finance & Legal Director + 7 Finance/Legal Specialists

## Usage
Run in any workspace:
```bash
/company Build a production-ready authentication flow with Flutter and Supabase
```
Or use natural language:
> "Delegate to the Company: Conduct a full security and performance audit of our API endpoints."
