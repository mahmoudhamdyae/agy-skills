# Task Lifecycle in the Virtual Company

```
[User Trigger: /company <task>]
               │
               ▼
   [Phase 1: CEO Intake & Routing]
   - Classify domain, scope, dependencies & risks
   - Output Standard Activation Report
               │
               ▼
   [Phase 2: Lead Handoff & Specialist Assignment]
   - Department Lead receives assignment contract
   - Specialist assigned with explicit acceptance criteria
               │
               ▼
   [Phase 3: Execution & Implementation]
   - Senior Specialist executes according to best practices
   - Runs verification, tests, and linter validation
               │
               ▼
   [Phase 4: Quality Review Gate]
   - Department Lead checks completeness & quality
   - QA / Security review for high-impact deliverables
               │
               ▼
   [Phase 5: CEO Synthesis & Executive Delivery]
   - Verify all acceptance criteria are met
   - Format and deliver final executive report to User
```
